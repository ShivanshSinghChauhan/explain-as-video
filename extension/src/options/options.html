Explain as Video — Settings

How to use it

  1. Highlight text on a web page.
  2. Click the floating Explain as video button (if enabled below), or right-click → Explain as video, or click the toolbar icon, or press Alt+Shift+E.
  3. Review the text in the new tab, then press Generate video.

Floating button

This needs Chrome's permission to run on all http/https sites. The button script only looks at your selection, and nothing is sent anywhere until you click it and confirm. Without it, use the right-click menu, the toolbar icon or the shortcut — those only touch the page you act on.

Storyboard engine

Before you add a key, please read:
  • Key storage: the key is saved unencrypted in this Chrome profile (chrome.storage.local). Anyone with access to this computer account, Chrome's developer tools, or malware on this machine could read it. Don't use this on shared computers. Create a dedicated key in Google AI Studio, and delete it there if you think it leaked.
  • What is sent: only the text you review and confirm (up to 6,000 characters) plus fixed instructions — never the rest of the page, its URL, or cookies. It goes directly from your browser to generativelanguage.googleapis.com; there is no other server.
  • Free tier data use: per Google's Gemini API terms, on the free (unpaid) tier Google may use your prompts and responses to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them. Don't send sensitive, confidential or personal information.
  • Limits and cost: the free tier has per-minute and per-day limits that Google sets per project, may change at any time, and are not guaranteed. When they run out you'll see a "rate limit" error; Demo mode still works. If you attach billing to the key's project, usage may be charged.
  • Region/age: Google's terms require users to be 18+; apps offered to users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland must use paid services.

About exported video

WebM export records the animated slides with burned-in captions. It has no audio track: the narration comes from your browser's speech synthesis, which plays through the operating system and cannot be captured by an extension. Download the .vtt/.srt captions for accessibility, or use a screen recorder with system audio if you need the voice in the file.
